Q. What color temperature fairy lights look best on TikTok and Reels?

Warm white 2700K–3000K copper-wire LED fairy lights create the most flattering golden background bokeh against neutral 4500K front key lighting.

Q. How far behind the subject should a Polaroid and fairy-light wall be?

Keeping 4 to 6 feet of distance between the model and the fairy-light wall allows an f/1.8–f/2.8 portrait lens to blur the bulbs into soft bokeh orbs.
